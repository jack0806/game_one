## 最终提示词

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: standalone transparent PNG sprite for the Hexblast game lobby's clickable deployment portal, shown about 330×330 px in a 1280×720 interface. Create ONE front-facing circular mechanical portal frame, orthographic 2D hand-painted sci-fi action cartoon style. Chunky rounded yet solid steel-blue armored segments, bold clean dark outlines, simplified broad highlights, cyan hex-energy seams and a few small warm gold fasteners. Match the game's sturdy comic-like industrial design and medium-bright readable lighting. Outer silhouette is a nearly circular segmented ring; central opening must be a large REAL transparent hole (at least half the diameter) so the game's separately animated glow is visible through it. Outside the ring is also truly transparent. Symmetric enough for a deploy gateway, but with distinctive modular panels rather than a generic smooth circle. Clear material and silhouette at 330 px. No ground, no environment, no cast shadow, no pedestal, no characters, no text, no logo, no UI border, no central energy vortex or filled center, no watermark.
